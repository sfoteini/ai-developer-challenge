        AI Developer Challenge #

        Get a solid foundation in machine learning and AI concepts and related Microsoft Azure services. This learning journey is designed for participants with both technical and non-technical backgrounds. In this challenge, you’ll learn how to create no-code predictive models, explore computer vision, natural language processing, conversational AI and so much more.

        Complete the challenge in time and you will get a chance of winning one of the prizes! Terms and Conditions →

        👉 Ready to get started? Start your learning journey →

        Why should you participate? #

        • Sharpen your technical skills: The 30-Day AI Developer Challenge can help you build skills and start your preparation for Microsoft Certifications for AI.

        • Compete: Track your progress against friends and coworkers.

        • Win: Complete the challenge in time and you will get a chance of winning a LinkedIn Premium voucher! See Terms and Conditions for eligibility details.

        Meet the organizer #

        Foteini Savvidou is an undergraduate student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (AUTH), Greece, affiliated with the Telecommunications Laboratory. She is interested in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Wireless Communications, Internet of Things (IoT), and Cloud Technologies, as well as in applications of technology in healthcare and education.

        In addition to her academic activities, she is actively involved in tech communities and constantly shares her knowledge and experience in AI, IoT, and Azure technologies. She is currently Microsoft MVP in the AI category and Gold Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador. She has co-authored a Microsoft course, published several technical articles, and delivered sessions in various national and international technology conferences around the above-mentioned technical fields.
