        Terms & Conditions

        All eligible individuals who complete the 30-Day AI Developer Challenge will get a chance to win a 12-Month LinkedIn Premium voucher. The following terms and conditions apply.

        • One (1) LinkedIn Premium voucher will be awarded per 10 participants. A minimum of 15 participants must complete the challenge.

        • The vouchers will be raffled at random.

        • Challenge participants earn one (1) incentive raffle submission for every 10 modules completed. Limit one (1) voucher per person.

        Note for Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors: Ambassadors participating in the AI Developer Challenge are not eligible to receive the award.
